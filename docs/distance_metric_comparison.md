## Recommendation

All 145 chunk embeddings from `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` have norms ranging 1.000-1.000 (mean 1.000), i.e. they are effectively unit-length already. When vectors are unit-normalized, Cosine similarity and Dot Product become mathematically equivalent ranking-wise (their top-k overlap above should be at or near 3/3), while Euclidean distance ranks differently because it is sensitive to the vectors' absolute position, not just their angle.

**We recommend Cosine similarity** for SyllaBot's retrieval step: it's the standard choice for sentence-transformer embeddings, is scale-invariant (robust if we ever swap in an embedding model that doesn't output unit vectors), and is natively supported as the default distance space in Chroma, our chosen vector database (see `src/vector_store.py`).
